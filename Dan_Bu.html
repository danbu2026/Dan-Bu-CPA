Professional Summary

I am a CPA with over a decade of hands-on experience in fund accounting, financial reporting, and Controller-level operations — including roles at Goldman Sachs and Clarion Partners, where I currently work on a $33B open-ended industrial REIT that has operated since 2003.

My experience spans the full fund reporting mandate: NCREIF, IRR, XIRR, and equity multiple performance reporting; complex property-level and fund-level waterfall model design and forecasting; JV and operating unit reconciliations including full capital activity tracking; and audit, lender, and investor reporting at institutional scale. I have 5 years of supervisory experience managing small accounting teams, currently leading two accountants at Clarion.

What further differentiates my profile is a hands-on capability in Microsoft Power Platform and AI tools. I have automated NCREIF and performance reporting workflows end-to-end — reducing reporting cycle time by 50% — and built multiple working tools using Power BI and Claude AI that accountants use quarterly. These include automated financial dashboards, reconciliation trackers, close-cycle monitors, and workflow approval systems — all designed and built by me directly as a finance professional, not as an IT project. I bring this capability directly into the Controller function.
